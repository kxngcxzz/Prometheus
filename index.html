What is actually happening here

The type
Drawn to an offscreen 2D canvas and uploaded as a texture. It is real text — change the copy and the glass bends the new words. A generated video could not do that.
The glass
A signed distance field, not a model. A lens profile across a column whose edges are perturbed by fractal noise, so it drifts and never repeats.
Refraction
The surface normal comes from the gradient of that field. The texture is then sampled at an offset along the normal — which is what bending light through glass actually does.
Colour fringing
Red, green and blue are sampled at slightly different offsets. That is dispersion, and it is the single detail that makes it read as glass rather than as a blur.
Cost
One fullscreen quad, no 3D library, about 90 lines of GLSL. No Three.js — that would have been 600KB before writing anything.
Fallback
No WebGL, no JavaScript, or a refused context and the plain DOM copy stays on screen. Reduced motion freezes the drift and keeps the refraction.
